## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Proposed Solution
1. Detailed explanation of the proposed solution

FarmConnect is a digital marketplace that connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers through a single online platform. Farmers can upload their agricultural products, set prices, and sell directly to customers without depending on multiple intermediaries. The platform also provides product details, quantity availability, online ordering, and delivery support.

2. How it addresses the problem

The platform reduces the involvement of unnecessary middlemen by allowing farmers and buyers to communicate and trade directly. Farmers can receive better prices for their products, while consumers can purchase fresh agricultural products at affordable prices. It also improves transparency in pricing and reduces delays in the supply chain.

3. Innovation and uniqueness of the solution

FarmConnect uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) to forecast product demand, suggest suitable prices, and optimize delivery routes. It provides real-time product availability, direct communication, digital payments, and bulk-order facilities. These features make agricultural trading faster, smarter, and more transparent.

## Technical Approach
1. Technologies to be used (e.g. programming languages, frameworks, hardware)
- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript – To create an interactive and user-friendly website.
- Backend: Python and Django – To manage users, products, orders, and transactions.
- Database: MySQL – To store farmer, consumer, product, and order details.
- AI/ML: Python, Scikit-learn – To predict product demand and support price suggestions.
- Maps and Navigation: Google Maps API – To identify locations and optimize delivery routes.
- Payment Integration: UPI and payment gateway – To support secure digital payments.
2. Methodology and process for implementation (Flow Charts/Images/Working Prototype)

The system follows a simple process:
1. Farmers register and create their profiles.
2. Farmers upload product names, quantities, prices, and images.
3. Consumers and bulk buyers browse available products.
4. Buyers select products and place orders.
5. The system confirms orders and processes payments.
6. Logistics partners collect products from farmers.
7. AI suggests optimized delivery routes.
8. Products are delivered directly to buyers.
9. Farmers receive payments, and buyers provide feedback.

## Feasibility and Viability
1. Analysis of the feasibility of the idea

The proposed system is technically and economically feasible because it can be developed using existing technologies such as Python, Django, MySQL, and AI tools. It can operate through smartphones and computers. The platform can initially be implemented in selected villages and later expanded to different regions.

2. Potential challenges and risks
- Farmers may have limited digital knowledge.
- Poor internet connectivity in rural areas.
- Transportation and delivery difficulties.
- Product quality and quantity differences.
- Payment delays and online transaction issues.
- Difficulty in maintaining fresh agricultural products.
3. Strategies for overcoming these challenges
- Provide a simple interface in regional languages.
- Conduct basic digital training for farmers.
- Support offline order assistance through local service centers.
- Partner with trusted logistics providers.
- Introduce product quality verification.
- Use secure payment gateways and transaction tracking.
- Use AI-based route optimization to reduce delivery time and transportation costs.
